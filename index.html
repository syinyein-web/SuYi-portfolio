CastleDeck (NYP x AWS Hackathon 2026) Built

AI-powered student learning platform · Backend Engineer

CastleDeck is an AI-powered student learning platform designed to make academic revision more efficient, personalised and collaborative. It addresses the challenge of managing extensive learning materials by transforming lecture slides, PDFs and notes into structured revision resources.

AI Revision Generator: Students can generate structured summaries, interactive flashcards, quizzes, practice questions and mind maps from their learning materials, supporting active recall and independent learning.

Sharing Circle: A course-based student community where students can create posts, request study notes, ask academic questions, share resources and connect with coursemates.

My Contribution: I developed backend APIs and integrated AI-powered study recap generation, supporting the transformation of learning materials into structured revision resources.

MARS: OUTPOST ZERO (NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026) Built

Mars survival game · AI Mission Controller

A survival game about getting astronauts through life on Mars. You choose a character (Engineer, Scientist, Medical Officer, Botanist, Geologist, Chemist, Commander or Pilot), pick a mission site and objective, then make decisions each sol to keep oxygen, power, food and water up until rescue arrives. Events like solar storms and dust storms test every choice.

I built the AI Mission Controller. Players can ask it for advice, and it gives context-aware recommendations on resource management and risk, while the player makes the final call.

  • AI
  • Decision support
  • Simulation
